What it does when you're not looking

Every browser, every account

It blocks below the browser, so private windows, a different browser or a new user account don't get around it.

Adult sites, without a list

A family filter catches adult sites as you go. You see "Adult content" instead of the site's name, and the name is never written down.

The usual detours are closed

Secure DNS in the browser, public DNS servers and iCloud Private Relay can't route around it.

It repairs itself

Two background services watch each other. Edit a setting, delete a file or stop one, and the other puts it back within seconds.

A way out that takes a week

Unlocking starts a 7-day countdown, timed with the internet's clock, so changing your Mac's clock does nothing. Every site stays blocked the whole time.

The dots

Each day you're protected fills a dot. A night Barricade had to hold turns orange. They're the same dots you see in the menu bar and on the block page.

They never leave your Mac. Barricade has no account and sends nothing anywhere: your list, your visits and your settings stay on this computer.

Installing

Open the installer and enter your Mac's password. Barricade blocks the sites you choose, and you can add more any time from the menu bar without a password.

There's no remove button. That's the point: a site you blocked on a clear day stays blocked on a bad night. If you really want out, unlocking takes seven days.
